02. Two real sample teardowns

Not fictional demos. I tore down two live SaaS landing pages so you can judge the quality before you pay. (Shared as fair commentary — if either founder wants their page featured or removed, just ask.)

Sample A — LaunchFast

Overall 6.2/10

SaaS starter kit (Astro / Next.js / SvelteKit) · launchfa.st · Founder publicly reported ~1,500 visitors → 6 sales in 3 weeks. Classic "traffic but no conversion" case.

Copy clarity 7/10 CTA strength 6/10 Trust signals 5/10 Friction 6/10

1. The logo wall is a firehose, not proof (Trust)

~60 integration logos in the hero zone. Visitors scan, nobody reads. Cut to the 6–8 names your buyer already trusts (Stripe, Supabase, Vercel…) and move the rest to a docs page. More logos ≠ more trust.

2. "Save 48" — 48 what? (Copy clarity)

The pricing pitch says pick one framework for $49 "or grab all three for $99 and save 48". Missing the dollar sign makes a smart buyer pause — and pausing kills momentum at the buy moment.

3. The AI objection is raised, not crushed (CTA)

A whole section compares "Prompt it with AI: 2–4 weeks, $200+ in tool spend" vs LaunchFast. It legitimizes the alternative (just use AI) without a killer differentiator above it. Lead with the one thing AI can't give you: battle-tested webhooks and auth edge cases.

✍️ Suggested rewrite (above the fold)

Headline: "Ship your SaaS this week — auth, billing and infra already wired." Subhead: "LaunchFast is a production-ready starter kit for Astro, Next.js and SvelteKit. Clone it, connect your providers, deploy. One payment, yours forever." CTA: "Get the starter kit — $49".

Scores and notes are my own assessment from a public visit on Oct 9, 2026. Not affiliated with LaunchFast.

Sample B — Refine AI

Overall 6.5/10

AI-visibility (GEO) tracking SaaS · getrefine.ai · Strong concept, strong hero — but the page hides the price and asks for a call.

Copy clarity 8/10 CTA strength 6/10 Trust signals 5/10 Friction 6/10

1. "Trusted by brands and agencies" — which ones? (Trust)

The social-proof heading names nobody. For a tool that sells visibility, invisible customers are a contradiction. Even 3 named logos or one quoted result ("mentions up 40% in 30 days") would 10x this section.

2. No prices on the pricing decision (Friction)

Plans are named (Visibility / Autopilot / Scale) with "7-day free trial" but no dollar figures on the landing page itself — while the Scale tier pushes "Book A Demo". Every extra step before the trial costs you the impatient buyer. Publish the numbers.

3. Three CTAs compete in one viewport (CTA)

"Get Started", "Get Started" again, "Book A Demo" — the visitor has to decide which door is theirs before they've decided they want in. One primary CTA ("Start free trial"), demo as a quiet secondary link.

✍️ Suggested rewrite (above the fold)

Keep the headline — "Your clients speak to ChatGPT. Make sure it recommends you." is excellent. Subhead: "Refine tracks your brand across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, shows where competitors get cited instead of you, and writes the content that wins the mention. First report in 24 hours — free for 7 days." CTA: "See my AI visibility — free".

Scores and notes are my own assessment from a public visit on Oct 9, 2026. Not affiliated with Refine AI.
